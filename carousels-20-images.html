20 responsive image carousels

Every example has its own markup (different tags, real <img> elements) and its own JS block. Scroll, swipe or drag each one; the mouse wheel over a carousel moves it sideways and page scroll continues at the ends. Pictures are generated placeholders: replace each data-ph="n" with src="images/your-photo.jpg".
