About INZO Impex

INZO Impex is engaged in the trading and export of high-quality food and agro-based products. The company specializes in sourcing, processing, and international distribution of premium grains, with a core focus on maize and paddy. The company also deals in spices including cardamom, clove, and black pepper.

  • Direct sourcing from farming communities
  • Careful cleaning, grading and packing
  • Dependable documentation and shipping

Our Services

Processing
